Virat Kohli is one of the greatest cricket players of all time.
He was born on November 5, 1988, in Delhi, India.
Kohli made his international debut for India in 2008.
He is known for his aggressive batting style and exceptional fitness.
Kohli has scored over 26,000 international runs across all formats.
He has scored 80 international centuries, the second most in cricket history.
Kohli captained the Indian cricket team from 2014 to 2022.
He plays for Royal Challengers Bengaluru in the Indian Premier League.
Kohli won the ICC Cricket World Cup with India in 2011.
He has won multiple ICC awards including ICC Cricketer of the Decade.
Kohli is widely regarded as one of the best batsmen in the history of limited-overs cricket.
